How to label
  • Judge the app's answer against the reference answers from the QASPER annotators. One matching reference is enough.
  • Correct: gives the reference's content. Extra detail is fine if it does not contradict it.
  • Partially correct: part of the reference, or the right content mixed with something wrong.
  • Incorrect: wrong, or misses the point.
  • An answer saying the paper does not give this is correct only when an annotator marked the question unanswerable.
  • Grade the content only, not the page citations. The judge's verdicts are hidden so the labels stay blind.
Each tap saves straight away. A note is optional; use it for borderline cases.
