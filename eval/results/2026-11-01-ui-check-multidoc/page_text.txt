Methods: The first paper relies on traditional NER models and evaluation metrics, whereas the second paper uses multilingual language models and bilingual language models for zero-shot translation.
Findings: The first paper presents results on NER performance across multiple languages, while the second paper reports results on zero-shot translation tasks.
