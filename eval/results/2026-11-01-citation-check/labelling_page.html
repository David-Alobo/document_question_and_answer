How to label

Each card shows one statement from an app answer and the text of the pages it cites, exactly as the model saw them. Decide whether those passages back up the statement. You do not need to know whether the statement is true of the paper; only whether these passages say it.

  • Supported: everything the statement says about the paper is in the passages, or follows directly from them.
  • Partly: some of it is in the passages, but some is missing or goes further than they do.
  • Not supported: the passages do not say it, or say something different.
  • Ignore wording, the citation format and phrases such as "according to the document".
  • A statement that the paper does not give something is supported only if the passages are about that topic and do not give it.
Each tap saves straight away. A note is optional; use it for borderline cases.
